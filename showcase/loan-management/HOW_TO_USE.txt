LAMBA ENTERPRISES — LOAN MANAGEMENT SYSTEM (OFFLINE)
========================================================================

Welcome to your Offline Loan Management System. Everything is stored
locally on your computer — no internet connection is required.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
QUICK START GUIDE (HOW TO OPEN & RUN)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open the file: "index.html"
   Double-click on "index.html" to open it in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.

2. Connect to your Excel file & Assets Folder:
   Click the gold "⚡ Connect Local Excel" button in the top right (or in Settings),
   and select this "Loan Management" project folder / "loan_data.xlsx".
   Now, any payment or change you make in the web app will AUTOMATICALLY 
   save directly into "loan_data.xlsx" and the "assets/" folder on your computer!

------------------------------------------------------------------------
KEY FEATURES & HOW TO USE
------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. DASHBOARD:
   - View Total Disbursed Principal, Total Collected, Balance Left,
     Interest/Profit earned, and Recovery %.
   - See "Today & Overdue Collections" with 1-click "Collect" buttons.
   - See portfolio breakdown across different Groups/Areas.

2. BORROWERS & ASSETS FOLDERS:
   - View borrowers in big visual cards with their photos/faces,
     full address, phone number, and guarantor/witness details.
   - Physical documents are organized in "assets/<PersonID_Name>/".
   - Person ID is a rigid, permanent primary key (e.g. P001, R001, REG-102)
     matching your physical paper ledger register.
   - Switch between "🗂️ Cards View" and "📋 Table View" anytime.
   - Add new borrowers with the "➕ Add New Borrower" button (1-click creates KYC and dispatches Loan #1).

3. PAYMENT TRACKING & MISSED FEEDING:
   - Click "💰 Collect Payment" on any card or timeline installment.
   - Record exact Date Paid, Amount, Mode (Cash, UPI, Cheque), and Notes.
   - If an installment is unpaid, click "🔴 Missed (₹0)" to log missed status with reason notes.
   - The system automatically calculates on-time / late days and color-codes:
     * 🟢 GREEN  = Paid in full on time
     * 🟡 ORANGE = Partial payment or paid late
     * 🔴 RED    = Missed / Overdue payment

4. EARLY FORECLOSURE & SETTLEMENT ("🛑 Settle Early"):
   - If a borrower wants to clear their entire remaining balance at once,
     click "🛑 Settle Early" on their loan card.
   - Enter the lump-sum settlement amount and payment mode.
   - Clears debt to ₹0, marks status as Completed, and immediately unlocks
     the "🆕 Disburse Next Loan" button!

5. WHATSAPP PAYMENT REMINDERS:
   - Click "📲 WhatsApp" on any borrower card.
   - Generates a respectful, pre-filled Hindi/English reminder with installment #,
     due date, amount due, remaining balance, and overdue days.

6. PRINTABLE PASSBOOKS & RECEIPTS:
   - Open any borrower profile and click "🖨 Statement" to generate
     and print an official A4 Loan Passbook & Repayment Statement.

7. EXCEL FILE STRUCTURE ("loan_data.xlsx"):
   - Sheet 1: "Borrowers" (Customer directory with full KYC & witness info)
   - Sheet 2: "Loans" (Loan accounts, principal, weekly due, balances)
   - Sheet 3: "Payment_Matrix" (Visual weekly spreadsheet grid with payment date stamps)
   - Sheet 4: "Payment_Transactions" (Audit log with exact dates & receipt numbers)
   - Sheet 5: "Settings" (Company name and configurations)

8. BACKUP & EXPORT OPTIONS:
   - "Export Full Backup ZIP": Downloads master Excel + all photo folders.
   - "Export Assets Folders": Downloads clean profile folders for company records.
